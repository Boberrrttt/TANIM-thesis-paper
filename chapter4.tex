\section{Usability Evaluation}
	This section presents a rigorous usability evaluation of the developed system, employing a structured, user-centered assessment framework to measure its effectiveness, efficiency, and user satisfaction across identified user groups. The evaluation was conducted using purposefully selected participants, consisting of a total of ten (10) respondents, specifically seven (7) farmers as primary end-users and three (3) Department of Agriculture (DA) officials as administrative users. This 7:3 distribution ensures adequate representation of both operational and managerial perspectives in system interaction.
	
	A standardized instrument, the System Usability Scale (SUS), was utilized to obtain quantifiable measures of perceived usability, complemented by qualitative feedback to capture user experience nuances. The collected data were systematically analyzed to compute usability scores, interpret acceptability thresholds, and identify potential usability issues. This approach ensures that the evaluation is methodologically sound and aligned with established Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) practices, providing a reliable basis for assessing overall system usability and guiding subsequent design improvements.
